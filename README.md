# Template-Script-Reservoirs-Lake-Winnipeg-Basin
Example script for work associated with "Impacts of dam reservoirs on phosphorus and nitrogen loads and stoichiometry in a large river basin (Lake Winnipeg Basin, Canada–USA)" (Starling et al. IN INTERNAL REVIEW). 

Skeleton scripts walks through how WRTDS (EGRET and EGRETci) where used to calculate reduction or enrichment to the chemical load across reservoirs when inflow and outflow data is available. Includes methods used to collect and summarize metrics of CVc/CVq, the B parameters as described by Zhang et al. (2016) and the N:P molar ratio.  Also includes the method for summarizing annual hydrological timing and intensity based on the cummulative hydrograph.
